How it works

Each receipt photo is checked in two phases: first the printed total is read, then every line item is extracted and the signed sum must match that total exactly. A mismatch gets one automatic re-check. Receipts submit in sequential bounded batches; valid rows are logged even while others wait for review with their photos. With both options off, mismatched rows stay for review and nothing unverified is saved. Force-save adds exactly one clearly labeled placeholder line for the difference; auto-discount only ever adds a negative line when items exceed a readable total. Placeholder lines are always marked in Notes — review them afterward.
